# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Plain static HTML/CSS/JS in a single `index.html`, plus `bg.mp3`. Hosted from the GitHub repo.

## Users
Jessica Tanji, the birthday recipient, opening a personal link from a close friend. Device unknown: phone or laptop, sound on or off. She reads once, maybe returns.

## Product Purpose
A long, sincere birthday letter from a close friend to Jessica, delivered as a web page. Success: she reads it to the end and feels appreciated.

## Positioning
A one-off personal gift, not a product. The letter is written to one person, in one friend's voice.

## Operating Context
Sent as a link on her birthday. Background music (`bg.mp3`) starts on the first tap, with a mute control. The page opens with an envelope.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Single static page. The letter is long and repetitive by design (many short lines). The owner allowed light edits: trimming or reordering for pacing is fine, rewriting is not.

## Brand Commitments
Voice: warm, personal, a bit playful, from a close friend. Keep the envelope-opening moment and the music.

## Evidence on Hand
The letter text in `index.html` (git history on `main`). No photos or shared-memory assets supplied. Do not invent memories, names or photos.

## Product Principles
- The words are the product; design serves reading.
- Sincere, never gushing or corporate.
- Works with sound off, on a small screen.
- Finishing the letter should feel like an arrival, not a chore.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Readable contrast. Full keyboard use.
